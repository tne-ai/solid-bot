Problem

Every app follows the same unspoken rule — collect everything, keep it forever, monetise later. Not because developers are evil. Because that's just how the web works. Users hand over their data and have no idea what happens next.

Why it matters now

AI changed everything. The data collected "just in case" is now training material. Health logs. Emails. Social graphs. Every data point held is now a potential AI training asset — and users never consented. Regulators are catching up. Breaches are front-page news. Most developers already know this is wrong.

The fix

Solid flips the model at the architectural level. Not as a policy. Not as a promise. As a structural guarantee. Users keep their data in their own Pod. Apps read it with permission, never hold it. You cannot sell what you do not have. You cannot leak what was never yours.
